Inside a ranked match

Ten players, one life a round, first to seven. The app runs the queue, the veto and the result.

Bodybomb 5v5

  1. Sign in with Steam

    No new account. Your rank belongs to the account that plays it.

  2. Queue solo or as five

    Parties up to five, matched like for like, teams balanced on skill.

  3. Toss, then veto

    Captains call the coin. The winner takes the side or the first ban; bans cut the pool to one map.

  4. Play it, get rated

    Leave Bodycam closed: the app opens it when your match is found, drops you straight into the host's game, and puts both teams the way the lobby picked them. Nobody adds anybody and nobody types a code. The score is reported from inside the game.
