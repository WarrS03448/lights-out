Why this exists

Lights Out is free, unofficial, and built by Sam and two friends as a home for custom Bodycam gamemodes.

The short version

Hi, my name is Sam and I’ve been interested in Bodycam ever since it originally launched years ago.

It fell off my radar until a couple of weeks leading up to the Locked and Loaded update. I’ve logged over 100 hours in the game in the last couple of weeks and am loving it.

It truly is a generational leap in FPS mechanics. The one spot I feel it’s currently lacking in is gamemodes.

Me, along with 2 other friends, created Lights Out, completely free, to exist as a home for custom gamemodes and, in time, a community competitive/ranked system.

We are not in any way affiliated with Reissad Studio or any of the game’s developers.

If you’d like to contact me about anything, email contact@theneeb.com and I’ll see it. Feedback is appreciated!

What it costs you

Lights Out is free. Downloads do not require an account; online matchmaking and social features require sign-in and use the information described in our Privacy Policy.

Free forever

The whole arrangement

  • No money. No price, no ads, no premium tier.
  • Sign-in for online features. Use Steam or a Lights Out account. We do not receive your Steam password. A Lights Out account stores your email, display name and a protected password hash.
  • Data that runs the service. We store account and Steam identities, match results and rankings, friends, messages, reports and limited diagnostics. Some profile and ranking information is public. Network providers also process connection addresses.
  • Clear retention and deletion. Match chat normally expires after seven days; reports can extend it to 180 days. Friend and admin conversations keep the latest 500 messages. Accounts and some other records have no automatic expiry. See the full retention details.
  • Your choices. To request access, correction or deletion, email contact@theneeb.com. Requests are handled manually after ownership checks; signing out or uninstalling does not delete server records. See how privacy requests work.
  • Community gamemodes. This tool is for community play, not cheating. Its game changes can be removed through the app.
