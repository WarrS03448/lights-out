About

Why this exists

Lights Out is free, unofficial, and built by one person who wanted more to play in a game he couldn’t put down.

The short version

Hi, my name is Sam and I’ve been interested in Bodycam ever since it originally launched years ago.

It fell off my radar until a couple of weeks leading up to the Locked and Loaded update. I’ve logged over 100 hours in the game in the last couple of weeks and am loving it.

It truly is a generational leap in FPS mechanics. The one spot I feel it’s currently lacking in is gamemodes.

I created Lights Out, completely free, to exist as a home for custom gamemodes and, in time, a community competitive/ranked system.

I am not in any way affiliated with Reissad Studio or any of the game’s developers.

If you’d like to contact me about anything, email contact@theneeb.com and I’ll see it. Feedback is appreciated!

What it costs you

Nothing, and it stays that way. There is no account to make, no launcher to leave running, and nothing about you goes anywhere.

Free forever

The whole arrangement

  • No money. No price, no ads, no premium tier.
  • No account. Download it and it works.
  • No data. An anonymous install count per gamemode, so I know what’s being played. No hardware IDs, no player names.
  • No trace left behind. Uninstall removes the one file it added to the game.

Give it a go

Free, no account, and it explains exactly what it touches before you install anything.
