Cookies & Storage

What the website and app store on your device.

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Public website

The current public information and download pages do not set advertising or analytics cookies and do not include third-party advertising pixels. Fonts are served from this website. The account-creation form does not store your password or verification code in website cookies or browser storage; those fields are submitted directly to the Lights Out service over the account API. Lights Out does not record your connection IP address in its application logs, analytics or player records. Requests still disclose it to the hosting service, and Railway’s HTTP request logs include source IP addresses. Absence of cookies does not mean absence of personal-data processing. See the Privacy Policy for the distinction between application records and provider logs.

Administrator sign-in

The administrator area uses an essential cookie named hubadmin to maintain an authorized sign-in. It is limited to the administrator path, marked Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, and defaults to eight hours unless the server configuration changes. Logging out clears it. Browser controls can delete or block it, but blocking it prevents that sign-in from working.

Steam and external services

Gameplay also verifies the Steam account running Bodycam, including when you choose Lights Out sign-in without linking Steam; see Privacy. Steam sign-in takes you to Valve’s service, which may set its own cookies and has its own privacy practices. Following an external link is different from this website embedding that service. See Steam’s Privacy Policy.

Windows app storage

The app saves preferences, game-path configuration, downloaded files and a sign-in token locally. Steam sign-in tokens expire after 90 days on the server, with the period refreshed when the account is checked. Temporary Lights Out sign-ins expire after 12 hours; choosing Remember sign-in keeps that device signed in until sign-out or an account security change revokes it. Temporary Lights Out sign-ins are not saved as remembered credentials. Saved sign-ins and pending sign-out records are encrypted for the current Windows user using Windows account protection; the app does not fall back to plaintext storage. Signing out revokes the current session; interrupted or offline sign-outs are retried, and pending sign-outs cannot restore a login. Clearing local files or uninstalling does not by itself delete rankings, reports or other server records; see Privacy for requests.

Tracking choices

We do not currently use personal information for targeted advertising or sale. The public site does not vary its behavior in response to the legacy Do Not Track signal. There is no optional website tracking to turn on through a consent banner in the current implementation. Before introducing non-exempt storage or tracking, we must provide the controls and consent required by applicable law. Questions: contact@theneeb.com.
