# Lights Out 2.3.92

Adds a live Players registered count beside Live games. Steam sign-ins and
verified Lights Out accounts are combined, with linked sign-in methods counted
once. Historical totals use retained registration records. The label is available
in all seven supported languages.

The official Windows installer is digitally signed by Samuel Warren.
Bodybomb 5v5 remains at 1.0.28 and Capture the Flag at 1.0.4.

Lights Out is a community project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Reissad Studio.
Lights Out and its authored gamemode downloads are not cheats.
