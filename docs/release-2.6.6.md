# Lights Out 2.6.6

The client header now shows the number of registered Lights Out users and the
number registered for the tournament, replacing the online-player, queue and
live-game counts.

Both totals update automatically across client screens. Linked Steam and
Lights Out accounts count once in the Lights Out total. The tournament total
includes registered players who have not played yet. Unavailable totals show a
dash, and reconnecting waits for fresh data. The labels are available in all
seven supported languages.

Tournament rules, account ownership, matchmaking and game-mode packs are
unchanged.

Lights Out provides community game modes and matchmaking. Its purpose is not to
cheat, and the files it installs are not cheats. It is a community project, not
affiliated with or endorsed by Reissad Studio.
