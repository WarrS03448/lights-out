# Lights Out 3.0.0

A new look for the client: depth across every screen, and all 25 rank badges
redrawn.

- **Depth.** The client is lit from above. Panels and cards catch light on
  their top edge and rest on soft shadows, the red hero stands out from the
  screen beside it, and the window's header floats over the content. Every
  button is now a key that stands up and travels when you press it: Find match,
  Accept, the 5v5 and 1v1 mode toggles, Penalties and Competitive info, and
  every tab. Text boxes and sliders sit into the surface, and dialogs float
  over a dimmed, softened client.
- **Rank badges.** Every rank now looks like its name, and every rank grows
  through its three divisions in its own way. Rookie carries dog tags,
  Private a chevron, Soldier a helmet with crossed rifles, and Veteran a star
  medal wreathed in laurels. Operator wears night-vision goggles inside a
  lock-on reticle. Shadow is a hooded figure in a cloak, Nightmare a horned
  thing with teeth, and Spectre a drifting wraith. Reaper is a skull and a
  scythe. Ranks 1 to 4 now use military colours, and every badge picks up the
  new lighting.

Nothing moves: the depth is light and shadow only, apart from a few pixels
under the Find match key. Server code is unchanged. The 5v5 (1.0.30), 1v1
(1.0.4) and CTF (1.0.4) packs are unchanged.

Validation includes the full client test suite, and a render of every screen
from the release files compared against the design review.

Lights Out is an unofficial community project, not affiliated with or endorsed
by Reissad Studio. Its purpose is community gamemodes, not cheating, and its
downloads are not designed to provide cheats.
