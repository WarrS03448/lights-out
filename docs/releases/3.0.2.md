# Lights Out 3.0.2

This release stops buttons flickering while you hover over them.

- The tabs at the top, Cancel while searching for a match, and other
  buttons no longer flicker while the pointer rests on them. The client
  redraws its screens about once a second, and each redraw made the button
  under the pointer fade from its resting look back into its hover look.
  Now the tabs are only redrawn when one of them changes, and a button
  redrawn under the pointer shows its hover look straight away. Buttons
  still animate when you move onto them or press them.

The client is based on 3.0.1. Server code is unchanged. The 5v5 (1.0.30),
1v1 (1.0.4) and CTF (1.0.4) packs are unchanged.

Validation:

- a browser test that hovers a tab, Cancel while searching and the update
  button, then sends changed snapshots and checks every frame after each
  one: it fails on 3.0.1 and passes now
- the same check on a Settings button and the Friends button
- the dropdown checks from 3.0.1 and the full client test suite
- signature and frozen self-check verification

Lights Out is an unofficial community project, not affiliated with or endorsed
by Reissad Studio. Its purpose is community gamemodes, not cheating, and its
downloads are not designed to provide cheats.
