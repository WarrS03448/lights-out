# Lights Out 2.8.4

This release improves installation and update verification following reports of
Avast IDP.Generic and IDP.HELU.PSE91 detections.

- Update verification uses Windows' native signature APIs, with no PowerShell
  execution on the player's PC. Publisher, subscriber identity, timestamp,
  product/version and downgrade checks remain enforced.
- The updater checks the executable's signed role, preventing an uninstaller
  from being accepted as an update even if it has a valid publisher signature.
- The app and final installer remain signed. Internal uninstaller signing is
  explicitly disabled to preserve compatibility with older update verifiers.
  The release builder rejects unexpected signed internal components.
- Startup cleanup runs once and removes only recognized Lights Out update files.
- The release self-check verifies the signed application through the same native
  verifier shipped to players.

The source audit also covered first launch, temporary files, permissions, child
processes and packaging. The application retains its standard per-user install,
one-folder package with UPX disabled, and required WebView2 components. Its
installer does not change antivirus settings or take ownership of quarantined
files. There is no evidence that PowerShell caused the reported standalone
installation detection; the old verifier ran only when applying an in-app update.

These are preventive improvements. The exact Avast trigger and clearance under
Avast have not been established, and the affected player need not perform a
diagnostic comparison. Existing older apps still contain their old verifier until
updated; downloading this installer directly uses the revised package.

Lights Out is an unofficial community project, not affiliated with or endorsed
by Reissad Studio. Its purpose is community gamemodes, not cheating, and its
downloads are not designed to provide cheats.
