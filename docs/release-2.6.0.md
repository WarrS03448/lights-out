# Lights Out 2.6.0

This release adds the launch tournament, private messages and tournament support.

- The $500 USD launch tournament runs September 26 through September 28, 2026,
  starting and ending at 11 AM Central (CDT, UTC−5). Register in the Tournament tab.
- Compete on net RR from eligible ranked 5v5 matches. Five completed eligible
  matches are required for prizes. Late registration has no retroactive credit.
- Prizes are $250, $125, $75, $37 and $13. Players still tied after both
  tiebreakers each receive the full prize for their shared place, including ties
  at fifth. Total payouts may exceed the $500 base pool.
- The client and website show the schedule, countdowns, live leaders and final
  results. Recorded service outages extend the event by their duration.
- Tournament support tickets go to event administrators. Results remain
  provisional until review and confirmation. Payouts use Zelle, Venmo or PayPal.
- Use the mail icon to message friends and receive official Lights Out Admin
  messages. Conversations retain their history.
- Confirmed cheating cases on the official service can reverse affected match
  RR results for both teams. Corrected matches are identified in match history.
- Voiding a ten-player match now requires six player votes.
- The general registered-player count has been removed from the client top bar.

The signed Windows client, website and official service are updated together.
Bodybomb 5v5 remains 1.0.28 and Capture the Flag remains 1.0.4.

## Public source scope

The public source includes the client, tournament, messaging and ordinary
moderation tools. Hosted fair-play review and result-correction providers are
not distributed. Their source-build adapters explicitly report unavailable;
they do not produce a clean-player assessment or perform corrections. Standard
ban/unban persistence and public result annotations remain available.

Automated tests use isolated fixtures. They do not replace live multiplayer
validation. This release makes no guarantee that cheating can be detected.
